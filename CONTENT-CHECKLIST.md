# Swity se kya-kya maangna hai

Website ban chuki hai. Ab usme asli content bharna hai. Ye list priority ke
hisaab se hai — upar wali cheezein sabse zyada booking laati hain.

---

## 🔴 1. PHOTOS — sabse zaroori

Client 90% faisla photo dekh ke karta hai. Baaki sab baad me.

### Kitni chahiye (kam se kam 15-20)

| Category | Kitni | Kya dikhna chahiye |
|----------|-------|---------------------|
| Bridal | 5–6 | Full haath kohni tak, dulha-dulhan motif, paer |
| Arabic | 3–4 | Bel-buti, flowy floral |
| Party / Simple | 3–4 | Ek taraf ka halka design |
| Feet | 2–3 | Payal style |
| Kids | 1–2 | Chhote cute design |

### Photo kaisi honi chahiye

- **Khadi (portrait)** — site ka layout khadi photo ke liye bana hai
- **Din ki roshni me**, khidki ke paas — tubelight/flash me colour galat aata hai
- **Plain background** — bistar ki chadar, saada deewar. Bikhri hui cheezein na dikhein
- Haath **poora frame me**, focus sharp
- Chehra dikhana zaroori nahi (kai clients ko pasand nahi hota)

### ⭐ Ye sabse important baat — usko zaroor batana

**Sirf cone lagane ke turant baad wali photo mat bhejna.**

Client ko design se zyada **colour** ki fikr hoti hai. Isliye har design ki
**do photo** chahiye:

1. Cone lagane ke turant baad (design saaf dikhta hai)
2. **24–48 ghante baad ka gehra colour** ← ye wala convince karta hai

Purane kaam ki aisi photo mil jaye to sabse achha. "Colour kaisa aata hai"
wala sawaal isi se khatam ho jaata hai.

### Saath me har photo ka

- Kaunsi category hai
- Kaunsa occasion tha (shaadi / karwa chauth / sangeet...)
- Us design ka **rate** (agar gallery me rate dikhana hai)

### Do aur photos

- **Swity ki apni photo** — kaam karte hue. About section me lagegi.
  Client artist ka chehra dekh ke bharosa karta hai.
- **Ek chaudi photo** hero background ke liye (optional)

---

## 🔴 2. RATES — asli wale

Abhi site pe jo rates hain wo **maine sample daale the**. Unko badalna zaroori hai.

Har package ke liye poochho:

- Naam
- **Shuruaati rate** (₹ ... se shuru)
- Usme kya-kya milta hai (3–4 point)
- Kitna time lagta hai

### Bridal ka rate alag se poochho

Bridal usually **haath pe kitna upar tak** hai — uspe tay hota hai:

- Sirf panja (haath ka agla hissa)
- Kohni tak
- Kohni se upar / poora haath
- Paer alag se lagenge ya package me hain?

### Ye 5 sawaal aksar chhoot jaate hain — inhe zaroor poochho

1. **Travel charge** — sheher ke andar free? Bahar kitne km pe kitna?
2. **Advance** — booking pakki karne ko kitna lete ho? Baaki kab?
3. **Payment** — UPI chalega? UPI ID kya hai? Sirf cash?
4. **Ek din me kitni booking** le sakti ho?
5. **Bulk rate** — 10+ logo pe per person kitna? Extra artist ka intezaam hai?

---

## 🟠 3. DISCOUNT — dhyan se

Site pe kata hua price aur "25% OFF" badge dikh sakta hai. Par:

> ⚠️ **Jhootha "original price" mat dikhana.** Agar wo kabhi ₹4,500 leti hi
> nahi thi, to ₹4,500 kaat ke ₹3,500 dikhana galat hai. Consumer rules me bhi
> ye theek nahi, aur client ko pata chale to bharosa toot jaata hai.

Discount tabhi lagao jab wo sach me ho. Aise sach ke discount ho sakte hain:

- **Off-season** (March–October) — shaadi ka season nahi hota, tab kam rate
- **Group** — 5+ ya 10+ logo pe per person kam
- **Jaldi booking** — 1 mahina pehle book karne pe kam
- **Purane client** — dobara aane pe

Usse poochho: "in me se koi sach me deti ho? kitna?"

---

## 🟠 4. REVIEWS — 5 se 8 chahiye

Photos ke baad **yehi sabse zyada booking laate hain.**

Har review ke liye:

- Client ka naam (ya sirf pehla naam)
- Occasion (Bridal / Karwa Chauth / Sangeet...)
- Usne kya kaha (2–3 line)
- Kitne star

**Kahan se laayein:** purane clients ke WhatsApp me tareef ke message pade
honge — unke screenshot se hi text utha lo. Naam use karne se pehle unse
ek baar pooch lena.

Abhi site pe jo 5 reviews hain wo **sample hain, nakli naam ke saath** —
inhe hatana zaroori hai. Nakli review se client ko dhokha hota hai aur
pakde jaane pa business ko nuksaan.

---

## 🟡 5. ASLI NUMBERS

Site pe abhi ye likha hai (maine sample daale the):

- 5+ Saal ka Experience
- 200+ Bridal Mehendi
- 1200+ Happy Clients
- 100% Natural Henna

**Inko asli karna zaroori hai.** Swity se poochho:

- Kitne saal se kaam kar rahi ho? (sach me)
- Kitni bridal ki hain, motemoti?
- Kitne client hue honge, motemoti?

Agar asli number chhote hain to koi baat nahi — **2 saal, 40 bridal** likhna
1200 jhoot se kahin behtar hai. Client jhoot pakad leta hai.

---

## 🟡 6. APNE BAARE ME (About section)

- Mehendi kab aur kaise shuru ki? (chhoti si kahani)
- Training kahan se li? Koi certificate hai?
- **Cone khud banati ho?** Usme kya daalti ho? (henna powder, nimbu, cheeni,
  essential oil...) — ye bada selling point hai
- Black henna / PPD use nahi karti — ye zaroor confirm karna, aur likhna
- Har client ke liye naya cone? (hygiene)

---

## 🟡 7. KAAM-KAAJ KI BAATEIN

- **Service area** — kaun-kaun se gaon/sheher jaati ho? Kitni door tak?
- **Time** — subah kitne baje se raat kitne baje tak?
- **Kitne din pehle** booking chahiye? (party ke liye? bridal ke liye?)
- **Peak season** kaunse mahine? (Nov–Feb?)
- **Instagram** ka link
- **Email** (booking form ki email wali suvidha ke liye)
- Koi aur sewa? (nail art, bridal makeup, hair)

---

## 🟢 8. Baad ke liye (abhi zaroori nahi)

- Reels / video
- Google Business profile (local search me bahut madad karta hai)
- Aftercare card ka text jo client ko deti ho

# 📱 WhatsApp pe bhejne ke liye

Ek saath sab mat bhejna — 3 message me todo, warna jawab nahi aayega.

### Message 1 — Photos

```
Didi, tumhari website bana di hai! Ab usme tumhare asli design lagane hain.

Ho sake to 15-20 photo bhej do — bridal 5-6, arabic 3-4, simple/party 3-4,
paer ki 2-3, bachcho ki 1-2.

Photo kaise ho:
- din ki roshni me, khidki ke paas (tubelight me colour galat aata hai)
- saada background (chadar/deewar)
- khadi photo, haath poora aa jaye

Aur ek khaas baat — sirf cone lagane ke baad wali nahi,
DO-TEEN DIN BAAD KE GEHRE COLOUR wali photo bhi bhejna.
Client design se zyada colour dekhta hai, wahi photo booking karwati hai.

Ek apni photo bhi bhejna, mehendi lagate hue — website pe tumhara
intro section hai.
```

### Message 2 — Rates

```
Ab rate bata do, har package ka:

1. Simple/party (dono haath ek taraf) - kitne se shuru?
2. Full haath (aage-peeche) - kitna?
3. Bridal - ye batana:
   - sirf panja kitna?
   - kohni tak kitna?
   - kohni se upar kitna?
   - paer alag se lagta hai ya package me?
4. Bacchon ka - kitna?
5. 10+ log ek saath - per person kitna?

Aur ye bhi:
- gaon/sheher se bahar jaane ka charge kitna? kitne km tak free?
- advance kitna leti ho?
- UPI chalta hai? UPI ID kya hai?
- ek din me kitni booking le sakti ho?
- kitne din pehle booking chahiye - party ke liye? bridal ke liye?
```

### Message 3 — Reviews aur baaki

```
Aakhri cheez -

1. Purane 5-6 client ke reviews chahiye. WhatsApp me kisi ne tareef ki ho
   to screenshot bhej do, main likh dunga. Naam use karne se pehle
   unse pooch lena.

2. Sach-sach batana (website pe likhunga):
   - kitne saal se kaam kar rahi ho?
   - motemoti kitni bridal ki hain?
   - motemoti kitne client hue?
   Chhota number ho to bhi chalega - jhoota bada number likhna theek nahi.

3. Cone khud banati ho? usme kya daalti ho?
   Black henna to use nahi karti na?

4. Instagram ka link aur email bhej do.

5. Koi discount sach me deti ho? (off-season, group, jaldi booking)
   Jo sach me deti ho wahi likhunga.
```

## Sab mil jaye to

Ye sab `admin.html` se site pe chadha denge — photo upload, rates, reviews,
numbers sab wahi se. Ya mujhe bhej dena, main daal dunga.

**Priority yaad rakhna:** photos > reviews > rates > baaki sab.
Agar sirf photos aur rates hi mil paye to bhi site kaam karne layak ho jayegi.
